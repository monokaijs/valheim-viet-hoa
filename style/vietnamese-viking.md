# Vietnamese Valheim voice guide

English is authoritative for meaning and mechanics. Vietnamese should feel written for the game,
not word-for-word converted.

- UI, controls, crafting, and status text: short, direct, familiar Vietnamese.
- Hugin, Munin, dreams, runestones, and lore: dignified saga voice; vivid but readable.
- Prefer strong concrete verbs and clear sentence rhythm. Avoid fake-old Vietnamese, excessive
  Hán-Việt vocabulary, internet slang, and added Viking clichés.
- Preserve who addresses whom. Do not invent gender, rank, kinship, motives, or lore.
- Keep proper names and recurring game terms consistent with `glossary.csv`.
- Never change `$tokens`, `$1` slots, format placeholders, rich-text tags, numbers, percentages,
  units, or control glyphs. These are executable game data, not prose.
- When a short label is ambiguous, inspect its key family, description, related rows, and official
  translations before choosing a Vietnamese term.
